Design System — Media Tracker

Light, quiet, and let the posters do the talking.

A warm paper-toned UI with no fixed brand color — every accent, glow, and highlight is pulled straight from the artwork of the title on screen. Rounded, friendly type. Balanced density. Built for fast logging, not spreadsheets.

Poster-driven color

Each title's dominant artwork color becomes its own ambient accent — a soft glow behind the poster, the highlight ring on hover, the tint on a rating chip. Move to the next title and the whole card's mood shifts with it. No two rows need to look the same.

When scraped art is missing or low-res, we never show a broken image or a loud placeholder icon — just a quiet blurred gradient in the neutral palette, so gaps in the catalog fade into the background instead of standing out.

Typography

Quicksand for headings — round, friendly, a little playful. Nunito Sans for everything else, kept plain and legible: titles, dates, and ratings never get cute.

Eyebrow / label — Nunito Sans 800 · 12px uppercase

Components

Rounded corners throughout (12–20px), generous tap targets, no drop shadows — depth comes from the paper/card tone shift, not shadows.
